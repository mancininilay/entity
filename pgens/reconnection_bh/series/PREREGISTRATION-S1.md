## 1. The question, and what the pair cannot show

**Does the loaded plasma of a plasmoid behave the same at twice the resolution?** At ten times the optical depth of R-low the pairs come level with the primaries inside 5 skin depths of the centre of a plasmoid, where the present grid has about 0.44 cell per skin depth of the mixture (0.26 inside 1 skin depth; shakedown report, section 19). At twice the resolution these are about 0.9 and 0.5. The pair compares, at the two resolutions and with three realisations each, the global state of the layer (inflow rate, spectra), the pairs in the cores (loading, density, energy, in the lab and in the frame of the pairs) and the delivered upstream.

It is a bounded sensitivity comparison of selected observables at two resolutions, with the filter and controller choices fixed below (Codex 1, review of stage C, C7; Codex 2, P7). It cannot show that the cores have converged, and a result without a flag is not evidence that the grids agree: a non-significant difference shows no equivalence. If neither grid develops the loaded regime (R0) it tests nothing about that regime. A pass at ten times the optical depth does not qualify thirty times or a run with cooling. No run without the policy is part of it, so it says nothing about what the pairs do to reconnection. The upstream is the one the rule `injector = 2` delivers, whose field weakens during a run (accepted by Nilay for this pair, and reported in every run).

**The grids differ in more than the cell size** (Codex 2, P7; series-preparation report, section 5): the time step halves; the horizontal block of the controller is 15.625 skin depths on both, but its vertical kernel (three rows against five) spans 0.732 against 0.610 skin depth, 16.7 percent less, with a second moment 25 percent smaller; the replenished regions begin 52.44 against 51.22 skin depths from each boundary in y; eight passes of the current filter reach half as far in skin depths on the fine grid; the particle-output stride is 5 against 20, so the fine grid writes about as many particles, not four times as many. A difference between the grids is a sensitivity to the mesh and the time step with these choices, not to the cell size alone.

**Members that are missing or partial.**
- *Preempted, or ended by a fault of the machine* (Slurm state PREEMPTED or NODE_FAIL; or a launcher, MPI start-up or device error before the engine's first step): resubmitted once, unchanged, into a fresh directory. If the second attempt also fails for such a reason, the member is missing.
- *Ended by the engine's time cap before its runtime:* a partial member. - *A crash, a non-finite value, an MPI capacity error, the probability gate, or the stop flag of `run.sh`:* a stop condition (section 6), not a fault of the machine; not resubmitted.

**The core series** (`core_series.py`, read by R0, R2a and R2b) uses one centre for the field and particle samples (the densest block of the pairs), selection blocks of fixed physical size on both grids, `size` as the equivalent radius sqrt(area / pi) of the plasmoid; it records, as diagnostics, the time offset between the field and particle outputs, the offset of the densest written leptons from the centre, the island history and its age, and the distance of the island's edge from the outlet. Plasmoids are matched by position only; size, age and outlet distance are reported per epoch and per bin, and nothing is claimed about controlling the history of a plasmoid.

**What a result can say.** A flag of R1 or R2a, or a fail of R2b, names a difference at the stated sensitivity. Their absence says "no dependence on the resolution was found at the stated sensitivity", with the intervals; it does not say "converged" or "equivalent".

## 6. Stop conditions

The pair stops on any of: a crash; a value that is not a number; an MPI capacity error; the start-up probability gate of the policy (largest probability per step over the tables 0.083 on the present grid and 0.041 on the fine one, against 0.1); the stop flag of `run.sh` (a value that is not a number, particle-steps above the tables, a pair dropped for capacity, a mean K of the leptons of the pairs above 175 with at least 1e5 of them, a species above 90 percent of its capacity on a rank; two readings in a row); and a registered check outside its tolerance: a fail of B, LF, C1, C2, C4 or C5 of any run that has them. The fine-grid release conditions 2 to 4 are not stop conditions; they hold the fine grid back (section 4). R0 to R3 and the reported quantities do not stop the pair.

**Stopping means:** no further job is submitted; the jobs of the pair that are pending or running are cancelled, with their outputs kept, except r-low-1card, which answers a separate question; the failure, its run and its numbers are reported; nothing is rerun with changed parameters. A run stopped by its own stop flag keeps the verdicts its checks give on what it wrote. Anything else needs a new sentence from Nilay.
